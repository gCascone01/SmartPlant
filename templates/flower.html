Instructions for Use

Here you can converse with the plant.

  • Type your message in the text field and press "Send" to submit.
  • Use the "Spray Leaves" button if you have misted the plant with water.
  • Press "Logout" to disconnect from the system.
  • If you are inactive for three minutes, you will be automatically disconnected.
  • Watering should be performed as close to the sensor as possible.
